# CenterPoint Connect — exploration report (JBK, Inc. Roofing Division)

Explored 2026-09-24, read-only, in the owner's already-logged-in Chrome session (user: Garry Peters). App version shown in the footer: 5.10.053630. Everything below is what was on screen or returned by the app's own data calls; nothing was edited, sent, exported or deleted. Where a number came from the app's backend rather than a screen, it is marked _(API)_. No login details appear in this file.

Two things to know before reading:

- CenterPoint stores tickets, projects and opportunities as one record type ("productions") with a `domain` of Service, Production or Opportunity. That is why some screens (Invoices, Schedule, Book of Business) mix them.
- The account has a bulk history import from March 2023 (invoices carry `productionImportId` / `importBatchId`; the earliest tickets are named "2", "4"…). So "3,759 tickets" ≠ 3,759 jobs worked inside CenterPoint; roughly 2,700 were imported at onboarding.

**Contact** (720): Name, Company, Position, Properties count, Email, Mobile Phone, Office Phone, Manager, "isBilling" flag _(API)_. Contacts are attached to a company and appear on all of that company's properties.

**Links.** A ticket points to a Property, a Bill-To company (`billedCompany`, may differ from the property's company), a Technician (employee), Site Contacts, and 0–n Invoices (`invoices`, `latestInvoice`). An invoice points back to its ticket (`productionId`, and the invoice number is the ticket number). The Invoices list shows OWNER / OWNER EXTERNAL ID and BILLING / BILLING EXTERNAL ID side by side, i.e. the property owner and the bill-to can be different companies, each with its external ID. One company → many properties (Bell County BOE 10, Pikeville Medical Center 29, 5K Inc 32).

**Quick Add** form fields (creates all four at once): Company Name*, Address, Timezone*, Company Account Manager; Property Name*, Address/Address 2/City/County/State/Zip, Timezone*, Building Number, Property Account Manager, Credit Hold?, Billing Instructions, Billing Address, Technician Instructions; Contact Full Name*, Position, Email, Mobile, Office Phone; Opportunity Lead Type (Eval Request, Existing Customer, Internet Request, Referral, Sales Call, Word of Mouth), Manager, Product (Bid / Repairs / Replacement), Sale Price, Description, Forecasted?, Opportunity Notes.

## 5. Invoices and payments

**Numbering.** Invoice # = ticket # (5431, 5449). Imported 2023 invoices carry the old job code as the number ("22R CTSKY 3", "21R HHWKY 1"). One invoice per ticket in everything seen (`isFinal` true; "Final" toggle on the editor).

**Invoice editor** (`#!/invoices/<id>`, from ticket "Edit & Send"). Tabs DETAILS / PAYMENTS / PURCHASE ORDERS. Invoice Information: Final, Invoice #, Invoice Date, Bill-To company, billing instructions; buttons **Send** and **View PDF**. Details: Amount Due, Produced (cost), Margin, Margin/Hour. Line table: DESCRIPTION | BILLABLE (qty × rate) | TOTAL | PRODUCED (qty × cost) | TOTAL | DATE, with icons for travel, labor, material. 5449's lines: Trace Floyd travel 3.5 h @ $55 = $192.50 (cost @ $85); Helper travel 3.5 h @ $40 = $140; Trace Floyd labor 8 h @ $85 = $680; Helper labor 8 h @ $55 = $440; Duro-Last 40 mil white membrane 136 SqFt @ $1.43 (cost $1.03); Term Bar 90 LF @ $0.80; DL 1½" P-3 screws 198 @ $0.158; Duro-Caulk White 12 @ $11 (cost $8.95); **Tax 0 % of $0.00 = $0.00**. Grand Total $1,882.26. Below the table is a live preview of the PDF.

Line items are generated from the ticket: each Timekeeping entry becomes a Travel or Labor line (with a matching "Helper" line when Number of Techs > 1) and each material used becomes a Material line priced at catalog cost × markup (account `materialMarkup` = 0.75, i.e. +75 %). Other fields stored on the invoice _(API)_: invoiceDate, dueDate (null), lastSentAt, isDraft, isFinal, paidAmount, dueAmount, subtotal, taxRate, taxAmount, isTaxExempt, costTotal, margin, jobHours, description (customer narrative), paymentTerms, checkoutName, billingAddress, propertyAddress, signature image URL, and the email that was sent (subject "Invoice #5431", message "Your Invoice is Ready!", three recipient contacts, attachments).

**Taxes.** Account: taxPercent 0, autoTaxMaterialOnInvoice off, autoTaxLaborOnInvoice off, label "Tax". Every invoice seen shows Tax $0.00; the imported ones are flagged isTaxExempt. No sales tax is computed in CenterPoint.

**Printed invoice** (`invoice.html?uuid=…`, screenshot saved). Page 1: JBK logo and address, "Service Invoice", Invoice #, Customer PO (218162), Invoice Date (08/10/26), Job # (26R YCES 2), Send To (bill-to, PO Box), Property, a one-line DESCRIPTION/AMOUNT table ("See Page 2 — $1,534.46"), Grand Total, Payment Terms "Payment is due upon receipt of invoice.", contact RoAnna Sims / sales@flatroofonline.com / (606) 523-9000, then the narrative description. Following pages: one "Work Completed" page per repair with the template text, "Completed 07/15/26", quantity, Before/After photos, and "Check In/Out With: NA".

**Payments.** The PAYMENTS tab has an "Add Payment" button; the Invoices list has PAID, PAID DATE and a ⋮ ▸ **Payment Import CSV** option. In practice payments are **not** recorded here: PAID is $0.00 on all 44 open invoices, "Total Paid: $0.00" in the list footer, and only **3 payment transactions exist in the whole account** _(API: $720 card 12/29/2023; two $47,955 check entries dated 4/29/2024 "Check 1669", entered 8/19/2025)_. Old invoices leave the list by being archived, not by being paid.

**Sage handoff — what was seen.** No Sage sync, no accounting-export button, and no "sent to accounting" flag anywhere in the UI. The only accounting-shaped things are: (a) the 6-digit **External Identifier** on 480 companies, shown on the Invoices list as OWNER EXTERNAL ID / BILLING EXTERNAL ID (this looks like a customer ID from another system — probably Sage — but nothing on screen says so); (b) the invoice-list CSV via "Email CSV"; (c) "Payment Import CSV". ## 12. Tasks and Opportunities in depth (added on request)

The team has used both lightly, but both are complete features. Here is what each can do, what has been used, and what a replacement would need if the team wants to lean on them more.

### Tasks & Meetings

**What exists.** A task is one record type with eight fixed types: To Do, Call, Drop, Inspection, Measure, Meeting, Property Approval, Repair Bid. The **New Task** form (`#!/tasks/new`, screenshot 49) has: Task* (type), Description, Due* (date + time), Multiple Days? toggle, Duration (None / 15 Min / 30 Min / 1 Hour / Custom), Employee* (owner), Attendees (other employees), Company, Property, With (a contact), Send Internal Calendar Invitation toggle, Send External Calendar Invitation toggle, and a Mark Complete box. Tasks & Meetings panels appear on every company, property, ticket, project and opportunity, so a task can hang off any record; the panel columns are TASK, DUE, TIME, DESCRIPTION, COMPLETED, EMPLOYEE, WITH, ATTENDEES, CONTACT, EMAIL, COMPANY, PROPERTY, OPPORTUNITY, COMPLETION NOTES. Completing a task stores "Completion Notes" and shows as "notes:task.completed" in the History & Notes feed (the bell's unread-mention count watches those). The **Calendar** (`#!/tasks`, screenshot 48) has today/week/month views and a left filter panel with four record groups — Book of Business, Service, Productions, Bids — each with a toggle that overlays that record type's dated items on the calendar (service ETAs, project schedules, bid due dates) alongside tasks; a people icon switches to a per-employee view. Task quotas per type feed the homepage "Quota / Compl." table and the Battle Stats report. "Record Complete Task" buttons on company and property cards log a completed task in one click. Service Agreements can auto-create inspection tasks and service tickets on a frequency (`autoScheduleTask`, `autoScheduleService`) — that is the only automation seen.

**What has been used** _(API, all 218 tasks)_: To Do 72, Inspection 58, Call 42, Property Approval 28, Repair Bid 9, Meeting 6, Measure 3. By year created: 2023: 16, 2024: 134, 2025: 51, 2026: 17. 108 of 218 are marked completed. Recent examples: "Senture – Monticello" (To Do), "Fall Maintenance after the leaves fall to all Knox County School" (To Do, due 10/30/26), "London Rentals – Roof Inspection" (Inspection, 9/1/26). Usage peaked in 2024 and has fallen off; nobody uses durations, attendees or calendar invitations on the tasks seen.

**Not in CenterPoint**: recurring tasks (other than via a Service Agreement), reminders/notifications for due tasks (only the internal mention count), task assignment to more than one owner, checklists or sub-tasks, and any link from a task to an invoice.

### Opportunities (leads, site bids, proposals)

**What exists.** An opportunity is the sales-side record. **New Opportunity** form (screenshot 60): Description, Company, Property, Sections (roof sections to bid), Lead Type (Eval Request, Existing Customer, Internet Request, Referral, Sales Call, Word of Mouth), Manager, Contacts, Product (Bid / Repairs / Replacement), Sale Price, Estimated Cost, Forecasted? toggle, Estimated Start Date, Close Date (Projected), Opportunity Notes. Quick Add creates one together with a new company/property/contact.

The opportunity page (screenshots 50–55) then offers, top to bottom:

- **Stages** — New Opp. → Project → Quoted → Sold (or Dead / Pending / Declined). When it converts with **Mark as Service** or **Mark as Project**, the _same record_ continues through the service or production stages, so opportunity 4510 shows New Opp. 4/4/24 → Project → Quoted 4/4 → Sold 4/9 → New Service 4/9 → Scheduled 6/13 → In Progress → Completed 6/14 → Authorized → Invoiced 6/17/24 on one strip. The opportunity ID becomes the ticket/project ID and the invoice number.
- **Site Bid Repairs** — repair lines (REPAIR / SECTION / QUANTITY / PRICE) from the 475-template catalog; "Include Repairs from Property" pulls repairs already captured on that property (from inspections or earlier tickets); "Capture Repair" adds one with photos; the ⋮ has bulk options. "Lump Sum on Site Bid: Yes" hides line prices and shows one amount (4510: two lines at $0 with a $3,250 sale price).
- **Site bid document** — View / Edit & Send produce a one-page "Site Bid No. 4510" (screenshot 56): customer block, Description, Amount, Work Description (free text), Terms and Conditions ("Upon completion an invoice will be submitted for Owner's remittance"), and a customer **Capture Signature** button with Date, Printed Name and PO# — i.e. e-sign acceptance on the tech's phone or by email link. Signed bids appear as "1 Signatures" in the file library.
- **Bid Status checklists** — "Customer / Job Overview" (Status*: Pending / Sales / Estimating / Estimate Complete / Order Complete; Company, Company Address, Property, Property Address, Property County, Sections to Bid, Project Point of Contact, Billing Contact, with an aerial map) and "Job Details" (Manufacturer, Manufacturer System, Bid Notes), each with a % complete bar.
- **Estimate Workflow ▸ Start Workflow** (a guided estimating wizard; not started, since it creates records), an **Estimate** line-item panel (TYPE / NAME / QTY / UNITS / COST / COST CODE / BUDGET TYPE / TOTAL / TAXABLE, "Add Estimate Item", "Generate Items from Repairs") and an Estimate summary (Materials, Labor, Other, Taxable, Tax In Cost, Tax Rate, Tax, Job Cost, Margin Rate, Markup Rate, Margin, Total, "Recalculate Totals").
- **Build a Bid ▸ Create Bid Document** — a page-builder proposal. Generated proposals are multi-page: cover with logo, "ROOF PROPOSAL", building photo, "Submitted to" block; a scope page with contact/property/date, "ROOF PROPOSED" narrative, Preparation, numbered Scope of Work, BASE PRICE, ALTERNATE add/deduct lines, Not Included, closing paragraph; and a Roof Diagram page (screenshots 58–59). The list tracks BID DOCUMENT, CREATED, SIGNATURE, SIGNED, PRINTED NAME, PURCHASE ORDER.
- Tasks & Meetings, History & Notes, File Library, as everywhere.

List-level: Opportunities list filters by stage-date month with Forecasted / Quoted / Sold / Open totals; ⋮ menu has Download CSV, Email CSV, Archive, **Assign Manager**, Choose Stage. Reports: Bid Report (36 bids: ID, product, manager, estimator, status, due date), Battle Stats (quoted/sold/close rate by product, weekly), Sales by Client (close rate per customer), Analytics funnel, Inventoried Repairs (emergency vs proactive repair value found per customer — for upselling inspections into repairs; all $0 today).

**What has been used** _(API)_: 86 opportunities (2023: 9, 2024: 57, 2025: 11, 2026: 9); 29 never left "New Opp."; about 15 reached Quoted and 11 Sold. 26 generated documents exist, all from Feb–Oct 2024: 16 "Proposal #…" (Build a Bid), 8 "Site Bid #…" (4510, 4610), 2 invoices. Only 4 proposal records and 21 estimate lines exist — the estimating side was tried in 2024 (Brandon Keck as estimator) and abandoned; the large 2025 Bell County replacements were quoted outside CenterPoint (their opportunities carry a sale price but no estimate or bid document). Repair site bids: 4510 is the only fully signed-and-invoiced example.

**Not in CenterPoint**: pipeline/kanban view (only a table by stage date), follow-up sequences or reminders, email tracking (sent proposals log only "lastSentAt"), multiple quote versions on one opportunity beyond "-2/-3" document copies, and any link from a bid document's price back into the invoice (4510's site bid lines are $0; the $3,250 lives only in Sale Price and the lump-sum document).

**If the team wants to do more here, the replacement needs**: (1) a lead record with type/source, manager, product, sale price, forecast flag and projected close, hanging off company + property; (2) a repair site bid built from captured repairs with photos, lump-sum or itemized, with a customer e-signature and PO capture that converts the bid straight into a service job (the 4510 pattern — this is the strongest feature they under-use); (3) a proposal document generator for replacements with cover/scope/alternates/diagram pages (already partly covered by the Estimator, so probably just a template step there); (4) tasks with the eight types, an owner, a related record, due date/time, completion notes, plus the things CenterPoint lacks — reminders, recurring tasks and a per-tech day view; (5) a calendar that overlays tasks with service ETAs and project dates, filtered by record group and employee; (6) the pipeline numbers (quoted / sold / close rate by product and by customer) that Battle Stats and Sales by Client provide.

### What this says about the replacement

If job costing is wanted later, the Materials/Inventory work already planned (truck stock → job) plus tech time on projects would give the labor/material actuals CenterPoint never got.
