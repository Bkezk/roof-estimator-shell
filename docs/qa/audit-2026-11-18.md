# Audit of the changes since Nov 16

Date: Nov 18, 2026

The owner's words: "we made quite a bit of changes can you go through audit everything thoroughly
to ensure it behaves as intended, don't assume, test everything and prove your findings".

Covered: the 25 changes from the Owner page clean-up (Nov 16) to the new Reminders page (Nov 18).
That is the margin report, inventory for year end, Trade Bids and their invoices, the Save bars,
ticket step notices and phone pushes, Special instructions, history at a property, the phone Tech
Board, the Claim button and the standard alert lines.

Nothing was changed by this audit. The findings went to the owner the same day and are not listed
here. Each one is written up with its fix when it is fixed.

## How it was done

1. The full check chain on the current code.
2. Five independent reads of the code, one per area, each told what the change was meant to do and
   asked for real defects only. Every finding passed on was checked again: by running the real
   code, by a trial on the live database that was rolled back, in a browser, or by reading the
   lines.
3. The live database: its functions against the saved files, its rules against the code's over a
   grid of cases, its access rules under four real roles, its triggers on temporary rows, and the
   real data for consistency.
4. The published site: which changes it has, and this morning's 8:00 reminder run.
5. A browser run of every changed page on the current code, on a desk and a phone size, as an
   admin, a manager, an office user and a technician-only login, with every save blocked.

## What was proved

- 594 test files, 8,474 checks pass. Lint, types and the production build are clean.
- The database's next-reminder rule and the code's give the same day and time in all 161,568 cases
  tried: 96 mixes of settings, 51 due dates in two windows that include both clock changes, 33
  starting days each, dated and undated work.
- The helper rules match on 13,624 cases: working days forward and back, working days between two
  days, 8:00 in the office for every day of 2026 and 2027, and the office's day across both clock
  changes.
- Four of the five database functions of the alerts change are the same as the saved file,
  character for character once spacing is evened out. The fifth is the same once three comment
  lines are left out.
- The new settings, the check on their numbers, the marks table, its read rule and its grants are
  on the live database as the file says.
- Under real roles on the live database, rolled back:
  - only an admin can change the reminder settings; a manager, an office user and a technician
    were refused;
  - a review pace of 0, a negative limit and a list in the wrong shape are refused by the database;
    blank boxes and 0 days are accepted;
  - nobody signed in can add, change or remove a "nobody on it" mark.
- On temporary rows, rolled back: a new ticket with no technician gets a mark; an edit keeps it; a
  technician put on removes it; a reassignment leaves it removed; taking the technician off makes
  a new one from that moment; an opportunity does the same. A dated ticket's first reminder, an
  opportunity's, and the next ones after a claim land on the working days the rule gives, at 8:00.
- Live data: every assigned open ticket and opportunity has its timer; every unassigned open one
  has its mark; no item has two timers; no timer points at a closed item.
- The published site has the changes up to the History pop-up and not the three after it. The
  alerts database change is live and the older published site runs correctly on it: the 8:00 run
  on Nov 18 sent the one reminder that was due, to the right person, with the right words, and set
  the next one three working days on at 8:00.
- The scheduled job is active on its schedule and its recent runs succeeded.
- Browser, saves blocked, no page errors and no failed reads:
  - an admin opens all twenty pages on a desk and a phone size;
  - a technician-only login is sent to My tickets from every office page, and other people's
    tickets read "Ticket not found";
  - a manager and an office user are kept out of Owner, Reminders and Users;
  - inventory shows no dollar values to a technician or an office user;
  - Work Overview has no Claim button where the row has "Assign to";
  - the phone Tech Board opens, a tap opens the details in place;
  - "History at this property" opens in place and a ticket's line opens its photos;
  - the ticket's box reads "Special instructions", and the Note form's "Viewable to technicians"
    box starts unticked;
  - the Reminders page shows its three cards and fourteen dropdowns.
- The phone board's code has not changed since its own touch tests on Nov 17.
- Trade bid sums were recomputed by hand and by script and match to the cent.

## Left as it was found

No temporary rows remain. Ticket numbering is untouched. The reminder settings were not saved. The
test login is as it was. The temporary proof file was removed from the code.

## Not tested

- Nothing was tried on a real iPhone or Android phone.
- No real email went out.
- No ticket was created or moved through the site, so nobody was sent a test notice.
- Two people doing the same thing at the same instant.
- The export files were not opened in Excel.
