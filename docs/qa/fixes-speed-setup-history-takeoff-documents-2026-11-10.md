## Decisions taken on judgment (for the owner to confirm)

1. **Office users and templates.** "Allow office users to have access" was read as: Documents
   access (Estimate, Invoices or Customers) is enough to manage templates, snippets and the PDF
   library. Before, that was a manager's.
2. **T-Bar goes in as No Drill**, since the sheet does not say; the notice tells the estimator
   to move it for a pre-drilled wall. A T-Bar row counted by the piece is left to place by hand.
3. **Picture links live one hour**, as before. The browser's kept list is emptied on any
   sign-out.
4. **The building search view.** Supabase's own checker will flag `buildings_search` as a view
   that runs as its owner. That is deliberate, and the view carries the table's read rule. Signed-in
   users hold SELECT on it and nothing else.
5. **A backup of the 217 old policies** is in `maint.policies_backup_20261110` (a private
   schema). `drop schema maint cascade` removes it once nobody needs the way back.

## Found on the way

- **The search view was writable when first made.** Supabase gives signed-in users every right
  on a new view, and a view that runs as its owner can be written through, which would have
  skipped the buildings table's own insert, update and delete rules. Caught in review the same
  hour; every right but SELECT was taken away on the live database and in the migration, with a
  test. Nothing suggests it was used.
- **A saved bid read "Unsaved changes"** (old; the live site's old code does the same). Two
  causes, both fixed with tests (`bid-unsaved-after-save-nov10b.test.ts`):
  - "Save anyway" on the unusual-numbers question took the saved baseline a render before the
    waved-through list was in state, so the bid looked changed until a second Save.
  - "+ New Section" left the placeholder fastener spacing (18 / 12 / 6) on the section and
    priced it on that until the bid was next opened, which then "re-derived" it and left the
    bid unsaved. A new section now takes the lookup's design table, lap and spacing at once. On
    a 100 x 50 ft Duro-Last section the placeholder priced the bid $72.49 low ($17,126.60
    against $17,199.09).
  - Browser, temporary bid: a new section reads 15 at once; Save anyway leaves the bid saved;
    reopening shows no notice and stays saved.
- **Bids without a due date.** 15 live bids have none: 11 from before the rule (Oct 12), which
  were never blocked, and 4 copies made after it, because copying did not ask for a day until
  the Nov 9 fix reached the live site (the newest was copied on Nov 9 a few hours before it
  did). The live site's Copy popup asks now. The legacy Bid-Advantage import still creates a
  bid with no due date. Nothing was changed here; the owner decides.
