# Screens, Nov 23: QR pages under Setup, public requests with Opportunities, pictures on inspection rows

The owner, Nov 23: "currently the QR pages are not located under the setup tab and we need to
find somewhere else for the public requests. On inspection 6077 why is there an option to add
from ticket photos when theres not an associated ticket? that shouldn't be on there if theres no
associated ticket linked with that inspection. we should also be able to add pictures to checked
marked good items since we can then use that to generate the care plan reports."

Four changes. Every one was tried signed in on the local test server against the live records,
on a temporary inspection ticket and one temporary request, both removed afterwards.

## 1. QR pages is a tab of Setup

- Setup's strip has a sixth tab, **QR pages**, for admins and managers (the people who see the
  four service tabs). Office staff with Documents only still see the Documents tab alone.
- It is a page of its own, as Documents is: the address is still `/setup/qr`. The page now
  carries Setup's heading and strip with QR pages lit. `/setup?tab=qr` goes there.
- The separate "QR pages" row is gone from the menu. The Setup row is lit while the page is open.
- Who may open it has not changed: the central gate (`/setup` is a manager's and an admin's),
  the server and the database.

Tried: `/setup?tab=qr` landed on `/setup/qr` with the strip reading Service rates, Material
pricing, Inspection checklist, Vehicles & drivers, Documents, QR pages, the last one lit, and
Setup lit in the menu. A press on Documents from there opened `/admin/documents`; a press on QR
pages from Documents and from `/setup` opened `/setup/qr`. No Save was pressed on any Setup page.

## 2. Public requests live with Opportunities

- The list's address is now `/opportunities/requests`. The old `/customers/requests` still
  opens and sends people on, so notices already sent keep working.
- Its menu row is gone. The Opportunities row is lit on the new address.
- The Opportunities page has a **Public requests** button at the top, beside New opportunity.
  It always shows for the people who may handle requests. It carries the number waiting,
  "Public requests (2)", and is amber when that number is above zero.
- Rights are as they were: Customers access, checked by the gate, the server function and the
  database. A person who sees Opportunities but may not handle requests gets no button, and no
  question is asked of the server for them, so they see no error.
- The notice to admins and managers and the Needs attention row follow the new address by
  themselves: both read it from one place (`PUBLIC_REQUESTS_PATH`).
- The number on the button comes from a new read-only server function,
  `countWaitingPublicRequests`, which asks for the same access as the list.

Tried: with one temporary request waiting the button read "Public requests (1)", amber, and
opened the list with that request on it. With none it read "Public requests", plain.
`/customers/requests` landed on `/opportunities/requests` with Opportunities lit in the menu.
The temporary request was put in with its "told" stamp already set, so no notice went out.

## 3. "From ticket photos" on an inspection row

What it was: a button that offers a row the pictures already on the same inspection (loose ones,
and ones on another row). It never reached another ticket. It was always drawn, greyed out when
there was nothing to offer, and its words read as if another ticket were involved.

- It is now drawn only when the inspection has at least one picture that is not on that row yet.
  With none it is not there at all.
- It says what it is: **From this inspection's other pictures (3)**. The picker it opens is
  titled "This inspection's other pictures".

Tried: inspection #6077 (looked at, not changed; it has no pictures) shows ten rows, each with a
Pictures button, and no such button anywhere. On the temporary ticket, after two pictures were
put on one row, another row offered "From this inspection's other pictures (2)" and its picker
listed both, each marked "also on Seams".

## 4. A labelled Pictures button on every row

Every row could take pictures since Nov 20, but the button was a bare camera and was missed.

- Each row now ends with a button that says **Pictures**, and **Pictures (2)** once the row has
  some. It opens the same panel as before: the camera, pictures already on the phone, the
  thumbnails.
- Adding a picture to a good row leaves it good and asks for no note. Tried with the camera and
  with From my photos: the row stayed ticked good through both, with no note box.
- The buttons are wider with the word on, so the row now wraps by itself. The label keeps room
  for its words; where the label and the buttons do not fit side by side, the buttons take a
  line of their own, to the right. A label breaks only between words or after a slash.

## Reports and a good row's pictures

Nothing was changed in the document generator. What it does today:

- The one-tap inspection report starts from the "Inspection report" template. Its repair list
  holds the rows marked as an issue, each with its pictures under it when the document's Photos
  option is on. Rows marked good are not in it.
- A good row's pictures get into a report by hand: in the editor add a **Checklist item** block
  and pick the row. The picker lists the issue rows first, then every other row. One block for
  each row.
- The saved shape of the repair list already has a field for "every row, not only the issues".
  Nothing sets it and the editor has no switch for it.
